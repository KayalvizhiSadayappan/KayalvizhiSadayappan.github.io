About Me

Hi, I'm Kayal, an environmental modeler passionate about water. I apply expertise in modeling - both process-based and AI-driven - and data analytics to uncover how water quality and quantity respond to climate extremes and long‑term change. I believe that coupling modeling with large‑scale climate and environmental data will be central to creating more resilient and sustainable futures.
