About Me

Hi, I'm Kayal, an environmental engineer passionate about water. I apply expertise in data analytics and predictive modeling - both process-based and AI-driven - to uncover patterns and processes driving water quality and quantity, and communicate the findings effectively to diverse technical and non-technical audiences.

I believe that leveraging the growing streams of large-scale environmental data is essential to ensure a reliable water supply. My goal is to translate data into actionable insights that inform decisions and support sustainability.
